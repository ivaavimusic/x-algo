## Summary

### 1. Clear, Actionable Restriction Transparency in Under the Hood
As requested in #216, Under the Hood labels now provide creators with high-level, human-readable reasons, affected elements, and explicit recommendation surface impact without exposing internal classifier weights:
- **`under-the-hood/strato/lib/underTheHoodLabels.strato`**:
  - Enriched both `postLabels` and `accountLabels` with:
    - `reason`: high-level readable category (e.g., *"Sexually suggestive or explicit visual media detected under Adult Content policy"*, *"Automated spam or repetitive distribution pattern detected"*).
    - `restriction`: explicit distribution impact (e.g., *"Not recommended to non-followers; content warning applied"*).
    - `scope`: distinct classification boundary (`"Post-level"` vs `"Account-level"`).
    - `affectedTarget`: specific affected element (`"Media"`, `"Text"`, `"Link"`, `"Account profile"`, etc.).
  - Added helper accessors (`postLabelReason`, `postLabelRestriction`, `postLabelScope`, `postLabelAffectedTarget`, `accountLabel...`) and exported them in the Strato `Library`.
- **`under-the-hood/strato/columns/underTheHoodReport.User.strato`**:
  - Updated `postLabelRow` and `accountLabelRow` to include `reason`, `restriction`, `scope`, and `affectedTarget` in `reportJson`.
- **`under-the-hood/jetfuel/routes/_lib/report.ts`**:
  - Extended `PostLabel` and `AccountLabel` types and JSON parsing for `reason`, `restriction`, `scope`, `affectedTarget`, and `affectedMedia`.
- **`under-the-hood/jetfuel/routes/report.tsx`**:
  - Updated `LabelCard` to render scope badges (`Post-level` in blue, `Account-level` in amber), high-level reason rows, affected elements (e.g., `Affected element: Media` or `Image 2`), and specific restriction surfaces.
- **`under-the-hood/jetfuel/routes/_lib/mock.ts`**:
  - Updated mock data to showcase the enriched attributes matching the RFC specification.

---

### 2. Keep Post-Level Restrictions Focused on the Post
Addressed the cascade where a single post label turns into an account-wide suppression penalty that hinders subsequent compliant content:
- **`home-mixer/filters/oon_nsfw_simclusters_filter.rs`**:
  - Out-of-network recommendation dropping is now focused on candidates that actually carry sensitive/NSFW content or media. Compliant non-media posts from the creator are retained rather than being dropped unconditionally across the board.
  - Added comprehensive unit tests verifying filtering behavior for media, labeled, and compliant posts.
- **`home-mixer/models/candidate.rs`**:
  - Prevented stamping `SAFETY_BIT_AUTHOR_NSFW` in Phoenix neural candidate conversion for compliant non-media posts from authors with author-level historical flags, ensuring clean subsequent content receives fair candidate ranking.
  - Added unit tests for `to_recsys_candidate()` verifying `safety_label_mask` on compliant candidates.
- **`safety-label-user-agg/postToUserLabelRules.strato`**:
  - Configured `mediaOnly: true` on the `nsfw_high_precision` aggregation rule so that non-media posts are not counted toward visual media aggregation.
