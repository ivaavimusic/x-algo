## Technical Details

generate_world accepts num_topics from 2 through 16, but samples 2 through 4 user topics regardless of the available count. Two- and three-topic worlds can fail with IndexError while assigning preferences.

Bound the sampled number of user topics by min(4, num_topics). Worlds with at least four topics retain the same RNG interval.

Validation: `python -m pytest -q phoenix/tests/test_world_topic_count.py` on Python 3.13.5. The regression produced **6 failed, 6 passed** at the base revision and **12 passed** with this change.

Seeded synthetic-world contract tests for 2, 3, 4 and 16 topics across three seeds; no claim about recommendation quality or GPU training.
