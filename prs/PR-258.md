## Technical Details

Closes #253.

**TL;DR:** In `vm-ranker`, the service that scores and diversifies For You candidates before home-mixer's selector, one `NaN` score can crash the request. This fixes its ranking sorts and the shared `author_pool_counts` sort in `xai-value-model` so invalid scores are handled safely.

**Bug:** vm-ranker's sorts treat `NaN` as equal to every number, which isn't a consistent ordering, so Rust's sort can panic and fail the whole request before it reaches the selector fixed in #204.

**Fix:** Add `compare_scores_desc`, which sorts real scores high to low and puts `NaN`/`±inf` last, and use it in `author_pool_counts`, DPP, and the ranker-service debug sort. Normal scores sort the same as before. DPP also now leaves non-finite scores out of its candidate pool, so a `+inf` score can't take over selection when the pool has spare room. With the #253 repro harness, these sorts no longer panic on `NaN` input, and new unit tests cover the crash case. Prepared with AI assistance.
